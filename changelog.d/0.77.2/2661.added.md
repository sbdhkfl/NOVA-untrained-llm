```{change} soup learn: from-scratch training subsystem
:id: scratch-training
:tags: feature

New `soup learn` command group and `soup_cli.scratch` package: a genuinely
untrained causal-transformer model (no pretrained weights), a byte-level BPE
tokenizer trained from the learning material, an approval-gated dataset
pipeline with versioned manifests and provenance, a real training engine
with checkpointing and resume, evaluation (loss/perplexity), and remote-only
training on Modal (plan-only by default; no silent local fallback).
